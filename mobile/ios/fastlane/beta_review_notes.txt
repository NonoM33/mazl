MAZL is a dating app for the Jewish community. Sign in with "Continue with Apple" using any Apple ID, then complete the short profile (name, birth date, gender, who you are looking for). During this beta, Discover shows sample profiles so testers can try the experience before the community grows; testers are told so by e-mail. The selfie check (Profile > Settings > Profile verification) uses the front camera and stores no image. The app is optimised for iPhone, including the foldable iPhone (book and laptop poses).
